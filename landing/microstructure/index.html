Volume-Synchronized Probability of Toxicity (VPIN) quantifies institutional order flow imbalance: $$ \text{VPIN} = \frac{\sum_{\tau=1}^N |V_\tau^B - V_\tau^S|}{N \cdot V} $$ At critical VPIN thresholds ($> 0.70$), market makers widen spreads or withdraw liquidity to avoid adverse selection.
